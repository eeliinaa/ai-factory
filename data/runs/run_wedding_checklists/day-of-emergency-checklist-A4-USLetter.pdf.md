QUICK FIXES (Immediate one-step actions)
- Stain: blot with cold water, use stain remover pen; do NOT rub.
- Dress rip/tear: safety pins inside seam; emergency sewing kit for larger repairs.
- Hem issue: use double-sided tape or quick stitch.
- Shoe pain/blisters: moleskin or bandaid on problem area.
- Makeup smudge: blot then pat, use blotting sheet, touch-up powder.
- Headache/nausea: offer water, ibuprofen (confirm allergies), move to quiet area.
