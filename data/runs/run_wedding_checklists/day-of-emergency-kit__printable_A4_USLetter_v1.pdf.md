DAY-OF EMERGENCY KIT CHECKLIST
One-page printable. Grouped categories with checkboxes and short usage hints.

CLOTHING & ALTERATIONS
[ ] Safety pins (assorted) – quick fixes for seams
[ ] Clear fashion tape – keep hems in place
[ ] Sewing kit (needles, thread matching colors) – for small rips
[ ] Spare hosiery / socks – tears or runs
[ ] Spare shoes (comfortable) – for dancing or blisters

MEDICAL & HYGIENE
[ ] Pain reliever (ibuprofen/acetaminophen) – relieve headache
[ ] Antacid / stomach relief – for upset stomach
[ ] Band-aids (various sizes) – blisters and small cuts
[ ] Antiseptic wipes – clean small wounds
[ ] Allergy medication / epi-pen (if needed) – critical for allergies

STAINS & CLEANING
[ ] Stain remover pen / wipes – treat spills quickly
[ ] Tide To Go (or equivalent) – immediate spot treatment
[ ] Lint roller – remove pet hair and lint

TOOLS & TECH
[ ] Duct tape / clear tape – secure signs or fix props
[ ] Scissors / multi-tool – open packages, trim threads
[ ] Zip ties / safety pins – temporary fixes
[ ] Phone charger & power bank – keep phones charged

VENDOR & PAPERWORK
[ ] Vendor contracts & contact sheet – emergency reference
[ ] Cash envelopes for tips & payments – labeled by vendor
[ ] Printed timelines (x10) – give to vendors/wedding party

SNACKS & COMFORT
[ ] Bottled water (small bottles) – hydration
[ ] Non-messy snacks (granola bars) – quick energy

Notes: Keep kit with wedding coordinator or trusted attendant. Add venue-specific items (umbrellas, folding chairs).

Print note: Export A4 & US Letter sizes.
