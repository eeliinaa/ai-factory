Delivery Notes — How buyers receive and use files

1) Downloading
 - After purchase, download the ZIP file 'week-of-day-of-wedding-kit_final_bundle_v1.zip'.
 - Unzip the package to a folder on your computer.

2) Google Sheet access
 - Open 'vendor-contacts_template_v1.xlsx' in Excel or upload to Google Drive.
 - Open in Google Sheets and File → Make a copy.
 - Replace the placeholder link in this file with your sheet link before sharing: INSERT_GOOGLE_SHEET_LINK_HERE

3) Printing recommendations
 - For best print quality choose 300 DPI settings.
 - Use 'Fit to page' only if margins differ; otherwise print at 100% scale.
 - Use double-sided only for internal notes; timelines and checklists print best single-sided.

4) Fillable PDF tips
 - Open 'vendor-contact-sheet__fillable_A4_USLetter_v1.pdf' in Adobe Reader to fill and save.
 - If fields do not appear interactive, try opening in a different PDF viewer or use Acrobat Reader.

5) Support
For any missing files or issues, contact support@yourshop.com with your order number. Replace the Google Sheet placeholder link during your final review before sharing with vendors.
