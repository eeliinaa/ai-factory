How to Use
1) Open the Excel file and enter your wedding date in the wedding_date cell. The timeline and week numbers will map accordingly.
2) Use the "Vendors" tab to track contacts, deposits, balances, and due dates.
3) Use the weekly checklist for actionable tasks — assign responsibilities and update status as tasks complete.
4) Print the fillable PDF pages for vendor meetings or keep the spreadsheet in Google Sheets for live collaboration.
