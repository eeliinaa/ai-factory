# How to use your 30-Week Wedding Planner

Quick start (2 minutes):
1) Open the Excel file and enter your wedding date in the top-left cell labeled "wedding_date." This will help you map weeks and suggested deadlines.
2) Review the "Example Filled" tab to see an example timeline. Copy tasks that match your plan into the "30-Week Timeline" tab.
3) Use the "Vendors" tab to enter each vendor and their payment schedule. Print the Vendor Payments Tracker for meetings.

PDF vs Spreadsheet — when to use each:
- Fillable PDF: Best for printing and filling by hand or for a quick on-the-go checklist. Use the PDF cover and weekly checklist when meeting with vendors or for a physical wedding folder.
- Spreadsheet (XLSX / Google Sheets): Best for tracking formulas (budget remaining), updating status, and sharing with your partner or planner. Use the spreadsheet for live collaboration and auto-calculations.

Printing tips:
- Paper size: pick the file version for your region (A4 or US Letter). For best results, print at 100% scale and choose 'Fit to printable area' only if your printer requires it.
- For multiple checklists per page: select 'Print multiple pages per sheet' only for internal drafts. For meeting-ready copies, print one checklist per page.

Budget & Vendor tracking:
- Enter budget numbers in the Budget tab (category, budgeted, actual) — remaining amounts will calculate automatically.
- Add vendor deposits and due dates in the Vendors tab. Use the conditional formatting to show overdue balances.

Converting to Google Sheets:
- Upload the XLSX to Google Drive, open with Google Sheets, then File → Make a copy. See the included google-sheets-setup.txt for step-by-step instructions.

Common FAQs
Q: My dates look wrong after import. A: Check spreadsheet locale (File → Settings) and ensure your date format matches your region.
Q: Can I add more weeks? A: Yes. Duplicate the week rows in the "30-Week Timeline" tab and adjust week numbers and dates.
Q: Are there macros? A: No macros are used. The template uses only standard formulas so it works in Excel and Google Sheets.

Tips for effective use
- Weekly review: schedule a 15–30 minute weekly check-in to update statuses and move tasks between weeks.
- Delegate: use the 'assigned_to' column in the Weekly Checklist to make clear who is responsible for each task.
- Keep one printed binder for vendor meetings: printed Vendor Tracker, Budget Snapshot, and current week's checklist.
