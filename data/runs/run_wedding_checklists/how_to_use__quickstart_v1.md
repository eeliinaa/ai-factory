How to use your Week-of & Day-of Wedding Kit (Quick Start)

Overview
This bundle includes printable and editable planning assets to help you manage the final 30 days, coordinate vendors, and run the day-of smoothly. Files included: week-of checklist, day-of timeline, fillable vendor contact sheet (PDF), vendor contacts spreadsheet (.xlsx), example-filled preview, and emergency kit checklist.

Step-by-step
1) Customize vendor contacts
 - Open vendor-contacts_template_v1.xlsx in Excel or upload to Google Sheets and make a copy.
 - Replace example rows with your vendor details and fill arrival times and payment notes.
 - Save a copy and export a PDF for distribution if needed.

2) Finalize and print timelines
 - Use the day-of timeline template to fill confirmed times from your vendors and the venue.
 - Print 8–12 copies (for coordinator, photographer, venue manager, officiant, bridal party leads).
 - Export a digital PDF to email to vendors who prefer digital copies.

3) Use the week-of checklist
 - Review the 30-day tasks and mark completed items each day.
 - Share the key deadlines with your planner or maid-of-honor.

4) Prepare the emergency kit
 - Use the emergency kit checklist to assemble a small bag kept with your coordinator.
 - Include payments/tips in labeled envelopes.

Printing tips
 - For best results print at 300 DPI on standard A4 or US Letter paper.
 - Use 'Scale to Fit' or 100% scaling in your print dialog. Choose 'Fit to page' only if margins differ.

Google Sheets sharing
 - For collaborative editing, share the Google Sheets copy with the planner as Editor and vendors as Viewers or Commenters.
 - Replace the placeholder link in delivery notes with your sheet's share link before distribution.
