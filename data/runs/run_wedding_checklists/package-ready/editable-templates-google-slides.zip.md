README.md (brief):
1) Open google-slides-master.pptx in Google Slides by uploading.
2) Edit text directly on slides or edit the Master Slide for global changes.
3) To change colors, edit the theme or use suggested color tokens on slide 1.
4) Replace placeholder vendor names and phone numbers.
5) Download: File > Download > PDF Document (.pdf) and choose 'PDF Print' with 300 DPI settings in export.
