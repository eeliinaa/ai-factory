Product Summary

The 'Wedding Emergency Kit Printable Bundle' is a compact, editable digital pack designed for the person in charge of the day-of kit (maid of honor, wedding coordinator, bridesmaid). It includes print-ready checklists, pocket-sized reference cards, bag tags, a vendor contact sheet, and a packing checklist — all provided in both US Letter and A4 formats.

Buyers can quickly personalize vendor contacts and kit owner details using the included Google Slides/PPTX source, or import the files into Canva. The bundle is designed to be printed and assembled the same day, offering a practical, low-cost way to ensure the wedding day runs smoothly even when small emergencies happen.
